A letter, and a memory
From that Delhi match evening
🔒
Tingu ji, Okay first things first — your morning gift? I loved it. I genuinely don't know how you managed to make something that thoughtful before most people are even awake. The crossword with our words? The scrapbook? I kept going back to it. You're either incredibly sweet or you were up at 3 AM being incredibly sweet — either way, I'm not complaining. (Okay I'm a little bit complaining that you're this cute and I can't be there.) But today belongs to RCB. And to us. I keep thinking about that Delhi match — the one we watched together. Do you remember how comfortable that evening was? Just the two of us, legs stretched out, watching Virat do what Virat does. And every big moment — every boundary, every wicket — felt like an excuse. An excuse to be closer. An excuse to make the match feel even more ours. That's what you do, you know. You take ordinary things and make them feel like they belong to us specifically. RCB came back this season the way they always do — stubbornly, loudly, with Virat Kohli looking like he had a personal vendetta against every opposition bowler. And we were there for all of it. From that Delhi evening to last night. Jo bhi ho, jaisi bhi ho — meri ho. That's us. Through the close finishes and the nail-biters and the "they're definitely losing this one" moments. Ee sala cup namde, Tingu. Ours too.
— Garvit
