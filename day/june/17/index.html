I  ·  the park

jahan main subah ap ko good morning kehta tha

Subah subah walk pe video call lagi rehti thi. Log idhar udhar ghoom rahe hote the aur main beech mein khade hokar I love you bol deta tha, kabhi ek flying kiss bhi de deta. Kisi ko dekhna ho dekhe, mujhe farak nahi padta tha. Ap mere saamne nahi thi, par har subah wahi feel hoti thi jo ap ke paas hone par hoti hai.

II  ·  pehla kamra

jahan interview ke baad ap se ghanton baat hoti thi

Interview ke baad jab dimaag bhara hua hota tha, isi bed pe baith kar main ap ko sab sunata tha, ek ek baat. Ap ghanton sunti rehti thi, kabhi bore nahi hui. Itne patience se mera saath dene ke liye, ap ka shukriya kehna bhi kam lagta hai.

III  ·  doosra kamra

jahan baatein thodi shararti ho jaati thi

Yeh wala kamra apna ek alag kissa rakhta hai. Kuch baatein yahin likhi rehne dete hain, kyunki kuch yaadein sirf ap aur mere beech hi rehni chahiye. Bas itna kahunga, yahan hasi bhi thi aur sharma bhi gaye the hum dono.

IV  ·  mandir

jahan main ap ke liye shukriya kehta tha

Yahan aakar main bhagwan ka shukriya karta tha, har baar. Ap ke liye, in mauko ke liye, aur apne ghar walo ke liye, jismein ab ap bhi shaamil hain. Ap mujhe yaad rahengi har baar jab main haath jodunga.

V  ·  chulha

jahan main apne haathon ki chai dikhata tha

Yahan se main ap ko dikhata tha ki main khud ke liye kya banata hoon, chai se shuru. Chota sa moment tha, par mujhe accha lagta tha ap ko apni roz ki zindagi mein le jaana, even agar woh sirf chai banana hi tha.

VI  ·  balcony

jahan ek baar main khud hi lock ho gaya

Ap se baat karte karte itna khoya hua tha ki khud ko hi bahar lock kar liya. Aap hass rahi thi shayad us waqt, aur main wahin khada apni hi galti pe haste hue ap se baat karta raha. Chote chote pal hi to yaadgaar ban jaate hain.

VII  ·  corridor

jahan call kaatne mein sabse zyada time lagta tha

Final bye bolne ke liye main yahan sabse zyada der khada rehta tha. Ek baar to seedhiyon pe hi baith gaya, kyunki na ap call kaatne ko taiyar thi, na main. Yeh corridor ne hum dono ke kayi adhoore alvida dekhe hain.

ek baat aapse 🏠

ap yahan kabhi nahi aayi, phir bhi yeh ghar aap se khaali nahi raha

Yeh ghar main jaldi chodne wala hoon. Naye shehar, nayi shuruaat. Par jaane se pehle chahta tha ap ko yeh ghoom dikha doon, kyunki har kamre mein kahin na kahin ap thi, chahe ap ko khud pata na ho.

Park mein ap thi jab main subah blow kiss deta tha. Kamre mein ap thi jab main interview ke baad apna dil halka karta tha. Mandir mein ap thi jab main shukriya kehta tha. Balcony, gas, corridor, har jagah, ap kisi na kisi tarah maujood thi.

Toh jahan bhi main jaaun, jaante rahiye, ap mere saath chalti hain. Naya ghar bhi, ap ke bina khaali nahi hoga.
