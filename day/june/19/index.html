Yaad 01

Woh Table

The Irish House  •  3 April 2025

Yahi woh jagah hai. Isi table se shuru hua tha sab.

Aap jaanti hain na, kuch jagah ka koi logic nahi hota kyun special lagti hain. Yeh table kisi museum mein nahi hai, koi fancy setup nahi tha. Bas ek corner, ek cup, aur pehli baar thoda khulke baat hone ka mauka mila tha. 3 April wali shaam yaad hai na? Pingu ko toh yaad hai. Hamesha rahegi.

Ek baar ek plan tha. Aapko yahan coffee pilani thi. Abhi bhi hai woh plan.

Yaad 03

Woh Wala
Vada Pav

Mumbai ki sabse zaroori cheez

Yahan ka vada pav khilana zaroori tha. Ab bhi hai.

Mumbai mein itni jagah thi jaane ki, lekin pehle kaam tha aapko iska taste dena. Asli Mumbai ka ek bite. Koi fancy restaurant nahi, koi reservation nahi. Bas yeh wali theli, yeh wala vada pav, aur aapka reaction dekhna tha. Yeh bhi pending hai Tingu Ji. List mein hai.

Yaad 04

Fit Check
Spot

Woh jagah jahan Tingu sab se achi lagti

Perfect background. Perfect lighting. Aur perfect aap.

Pingu photographer nahi hai, yeh sab jaante hain. Par kuch jagah aise hote hain jahan ek photo leni hi padti hai. Yeh spot waisi hi jagah hai. Aapka ek photo yahan chahiye tha. Abhi bhi chahiye. Mumbai ne wait kiya hai, thoda aur karega.

"Kuch trips hote hain jo hue nahi hote,
phir bhi yaadein bana dete hain."

Aaj aap office mein hain aur main yahan hoon. Par yeh sab jagah hain jo abhi bhi wait kar rahi hain humare liye. Irish House wali table, Blue Tokai ki coffee, ek vada pav ki queue, aur woh ek perfect fit check. Sab kuch hoga, Tingu Ji. Ek ek karke.

Abhi ke liye, yeh page aapka hai. Ek reminder ki Mumbai mein ek Pingu hai, jo soch raha hai aapke baare mein.
